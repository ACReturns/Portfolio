Turtle Tester⁠→

Turtle Rock Studios · internal tool

  • C#
  • Jenkins
  • PlayStation & Xbox SDKs
  • Jira API

One app that replaced a chain of manual steps. It listed every Jenkins build with its branch, size and version, downloaded it, and pushed it straight to PS4/PS5 or Xbox One/Series X.

From inside the game, testers filed Jira bugs with logs captured at the exact moment of the issue, and sent debug inputs back to the build.

Finding the right build for the right platform used to take hours. With Turtle Tester it happened in one place.
